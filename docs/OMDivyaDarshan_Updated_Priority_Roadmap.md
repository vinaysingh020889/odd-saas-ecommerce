# 1. Updated Platform Direction

The platform architecture remains unchanged:

-   **www.omdivyadarshan.org** → WordPress Discovery Layer
-   **shop.omdivyadarshan.org** → Commerce & Customer Application
-   **admin.omdivyadarshan.org** → Admin Control Panel
-   **wallet.omdivyadarshan.org** → Wallet & Rewards (Future Phase)
-   **api.omdivyadarshan.org** → Central Backend APIs

WordPress remains responsible for discovery, SEO, landing pages and
trust building.

The Shop remains the transactional application.

## Membership-First Commerce

The Shop becomes membership driven.

### Browsing

Users may browse products without logging in.

### Purchase Rule

Before checkout every customer must activate a membership.

Minimum requirement:

-   Free Membership

Paid memberships unlock:

-   Free service credits
-   Free Prasad eligibility
-   Exclusive offers
-   Higher discounts
-   Priority queue
-   Kundli benefits
-   Travel package information
-   Future loyalty benefits

Membership becomes the gateway to commerce.
